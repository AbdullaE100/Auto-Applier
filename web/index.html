FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will JobFlow make things up on my applications?

No. Answers come only from your CV, your questionnaire answers and answers you've saved. When JobFlow isn't sure, it leaves the question for you and remembers what you type.

Does it submit applications without me?

By default JobFlow fills everything and stops on the final review screen so you click Submit. You can switch on auto-submit in settings if you prefer.

Is this allowed by LinkedIn?

LinkedIn's terms don't allow automated tools. JobFlow runs in your own browser at a human pace with daily limits and review mode, but you're responsible for how you use it on any site.

Which sites does it work on?

LinkedIn Easy Apply, plus one-click autofill on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable career pages.

What happens to my CV and data?

Your CV is stored privately and only you can access it. Text from your CV is sent to our AI provider to extract details and draft answers. We never sell your data. See the privacy policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Export your data from the dashboard at any time, or request deletion from Settings and we'll remove everything within 30 days.
